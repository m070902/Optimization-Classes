# Optimization Classes
In this repository different optimization exercises have been solved based on the curriculum of the course "Optimización en Sistemas Físicos y Aplicaciones Industriales" of the master in physics "Tecnología Física: Investigación y aplicaciones" offered by the University of Córdoba.

The content of the exercises are the following:
1. Introduction to optimization problems. Solving the Traveling Salesman Problem.
2. Physical and mathematical principles of optimization algorithms. Random numbers generation.
3. Optimization algorithms in high dimensionality spaces. Simmulated Annealing.
